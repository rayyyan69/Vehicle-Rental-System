About This Project

DriveEasy is a Vehicle Rental Management System built as a frontend student project. It shows how a small rental business could manage:

🚗

Vehicle Information

Name, brand, type, model, registration and price.

👤

Customer Details

Auto-generated customer IDs with basic contact info.

📅

Rental Bookings

Book, view and return vehicles with a full history.

✅

Availability

Rented vehicles cannot be booked again until returned.

🧮

Rental Calculation

Total = price per day × number of days.

Demo only: data is saved in your browser's localStorage, which is not a secure database. No real bookings, payments or licence verification happen here.
